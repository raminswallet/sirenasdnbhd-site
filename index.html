The Sirena approach

Connecting relevant requirements with selected supply programmes.

Sirena Sdn Bhd supports international B2B seafood trade involving frozen products, processing raw material and selected marine specialities. We consider both qualified buying requirements and relevant supply opportunities across established international seafood markets.
Species, origin, catch area, product form, grade, packing, allocation, vessel or plant details, documentation, pricing and shipment timing remain subject to confirmation by the relevant producer, exporter, processor, vessel owner or appointed representative.
Selected seafood focus

Broad product coverage, assessed opportunity by opportunity.

Tuna & Large Pelagics

Skipjack, yellowfin, bigeye, albacore, longtail tuna, swordfish, marlin and selected oceanic species.

Wahoo, Seerfish & King Mackerel

Wahoo, Spanish mackerel, seerfish, king mackerel and related high-value pelagic programmes.

Mackerel & Pelagic Fish

Blue, Atlantic and chub mackerel; jack and horse mackerel; scad, sardine, anchovy and bonito.

Whitefish & Groundfish

Hake, hoki, ling, cod, pollock, haddock, blue whiting, redfish, saithe and selected groundfish.

Flatfish & Demersal Fish

Cephalopods & Shellfish

Squid, cuttlefish, octopus, selected shrimp, prawns, crab, scallops, roe and marine specialities.
